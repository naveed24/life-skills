# Tiny Habits

### Question 1: In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

I liked the idea of starting by flossing just one tooth after brushing.  
It shows how a very small action can slowly turn into a bigger habit.

### Question 2: How can you use B = MAP to make new habits easier? What are M, A and P?

M means motivation, A means ability and P means prompt.  
I can build a habit by choosing something I want to do, making it easy and setting a reminder.

### Question 3: Why is it important to "Shine" or celebrate after completing a habit?

Celebrating a small achievement makes us feel good about what we have done.  
This positive feeling makes us more likely to repeat the habit.

### Question 4: In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

I liked how the British cycling team improved by making small changes in many areas.  
It shows that small improvements over time can lead to big results.

### Question 5: What is the book's perspective about identity?

Instead of focusing only on a goal, we should focus on the person we want to become.  
Every small action we take helps us become that person.

### Question 6: What is the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?

We should make good habits noticeable, enjoyable, easy to do and rewarding.  
Starting with a small action and removing obstacles makes it easier to stay consistent.

### Question 7: What is the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?

We should hide the reminders of bad habits and make them less appealing.  
Making them difficult to do and less rewarding helps us avoid them.

### Question 8: Pick one habit that you would like to do more of. What steps can you take to build it?

I want to practise coding daily, so I will open my code editor after breakfast and start with one small problem.  
I will mark my progress on a calendar after completing it to feel good about my effort.

### Question 9: Pick one habit that you would like to eliminate or do less of. What steps can you take to reduce it?

I want to spend less time scrolling on social media, so I will turn off notifications and remove the apps from my home screen.  
I will keep my phone away while studying and set a daily time limit for social media.
