# Listening and Assertive Communication

## Question 1: What are the steps or strategies for active listening?

1. Give the speaker your full attention. 2. Maintain comfortable eye contact.
3. Avoid interrupting. 4. Pay attention to their tone and body language.
5. Ask questions if something is unclear. 6. Repeat the main point in your own words to check your understanding.

## Question 2: According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of reflective listening?

Fisher's model says we should try to understand things from the speaker's point of view. It includes empathy, accepting others without judging, being genuine, and focusing on clear details.
We should listen more than we talk and repeat what we understood, including their feelings, so they can correct us if needed.

## Question 3: What are the obstacles in your listening process?

Sometimes I get distracted by my phone or start thinking about what to reply before the other person finishes.
If the discussion is long, I also find it hard to remember every important point.

## Question 4: What can you do to improve your listening?

I can keep my phone aside, pay attention, and let the other person finish before I respond.
During meetings, I can take short notes and ask questions whenever I don't understand something.

## Question 5: When do you switch to a passive communication style in daily life?

I tend to stay quiet when someone senior disagrees with me, even when I have a different opinion.
Sometimes I agree just to avoid an uncomfortable conversation.

## Question 6: When do you switch to an aggressive communication style in daily life?

When I am stressed or feel that someone is not listening to me, I sometimes become impatient and raise my voice.
Later, I realise that speaking calmly would have helped more.

## Question 7: When do you switch to a passive-aggressive communication style in daily life?

When something upsets me, I sometimes go quiet or give short replies instead of explaining the problem.
I know it is better to say what is bothering me rather than expect others to guess.

## Question 8: How can you make your communication more assertive?

I can explain my thoughts clearly and calmly using phrases like "I feel" or "I think" instead of blaming others.
I also want to practise saying no politely, listening to other opinions, and discussing problems directly.
