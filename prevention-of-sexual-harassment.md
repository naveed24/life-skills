# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes unwanted touching, inappropriate comments or jokes, sharing sexual messages or pictures, and repeatedly asking someone out after they say no. Any unwanted sexual behaviour that makes a person feel uncomfortable or unsafe is not acceptable, whether it happens in person or online.

## 2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

If I face sexual harassment, I will speak up if it is safe, save any evidence, and report it to HR or the Internal Committee. If I witness it, I will support the affected person and report what I saw. I will respect their privacy and not ignore the incident.
